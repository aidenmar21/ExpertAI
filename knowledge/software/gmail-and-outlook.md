# Gmail and Outlook
The inbox used as a work queue: where invoices, tickets, guest requests, new-hire paperwork and booking requests arrive, get triaged, and get answered.

## 1. What it is and who uses it
Gmail (Google Workspace) and Outlook (Microsoft 365, desktop "classic" and "new" Outlook, and Outlook on the web) are the first screen most desk roles open and the last they close. Support agents, AP clerks, receptionists, HR coordinators, schedulers and bookkeepers all use mail as the intake channel and as the audit trail of who promised what. A shared mailbox (ap@, support@, frontdesk@, hr@) is the team's queue; the personal inbox is for things addressed to you. Calendar is attached to mail in both products, so invites arrive and get accepted here.

## 2. Main screens and objects
- **Inbox** list (Gmail: Primary, Promotions, Social, Updates category tabs; Outlook: Focused / Other) and the **Reading pane** on the right.
- **Message / Conversation thread**: Gmail groups replies into one conversation; Outlook does too when Conversation view is on, otherwise each reply is a separate row.
- **Labels** (Gmail, many per message, colour-coded chips) versus **Folders** and **Categories** (Outlook: one folder, many coloured categories).
- **Shared mailbox** (Outlook, appears as a second account tree) or **Google Group / collaborative inbox** (Gmail delegated mailbox).
- **Compose window** with To, Cc, Bcc, Subject, body, attachment paperclip, signature.
- **Drafts**, **Sent**, **Snoozed** (Gmail) / **Scheduled** (Outlook: Delay Delivery), **Spam/Junk**, **Trash/Deleted Items**, **Archive** (Gmail: All Mail; Outlook: Archive folder).
- **Rules** (Outlook: Settings > Mail > Rules) and **Filters** (Gmail: Settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses).
- **Templates** (Gmail: Settings > Advanced > Templates, inserted from the three-dot menu in Compose) and **Quick Parts / My Templates** (Outlook).
- **Calendar invite card** inside a message with Yes / Maybe / No (Gmail) or Accept / Tentative / Decline (Outlook).
- **Search bar** with chips (From, Any time, Has attachment, Is unread) and operators.

## 3. Field names and statuses you would see
- Message row: sender name, subject, snippet, time or date, paperclip (attachment), star (Gmail) or flag (Outlook), label chips, importance marker (Gmail yellow arrow), red exclamation (Outlook High importance), blue dot or bold text (unread), reply arrow and forward arrow icons on read messages.
- Header fields: From, To, Cc, Bcc, Date, Subject, Reply-To, "via" (Gmail, sent on behalf), "Sent on behalf of" (Outlook shared mailbox).
- Gmail-specific labels and statuses: Inbox, Starred, Snoozed, Important, Sent, Drafts, Spam, Trash, All Mail, Scheduled, custom labels like `AP/To process`, `AP/Waiting on supplier`, `Support/Escalated`. Snooze shows "Snoozed until Mon, 9:00 AM".
- Outlook-specific: Flag states (Flagged, Completed with a tick, Today, Tomorrow, This week, No date), Categories (Red category, "Urgent", "Waiting", "Invoice"), Follow Up reminder, Read receipt requested, Tracking tab on a sent item (Read / Not read / Delivered), Delay Delivery time, Sensitivity (Normal, Personal, Private, Confidential), Encrypt / Do Not Forward banner, "This message was sent with High importance".
- Mailbox warnings and banners: "This message seems dangerous", "External" tag on the sender, "You replied on 3 Oct", "This message has been forwarded", "Recipient's mailbox is full", "Undeliverable" / "Mail Delivery Subsystem" bounce, "Out of Office" auto-reply, "Message recalled" notice (Outlook).
- Attachment chips: filename.pdf, size, preview icon; Google Drive link chip; "Attachment blocked" for .exe/.js.
- Invite card: event title, date and time, location/Meet or Teams link, "Going?" Yes / No / Maybe, or Accept / Tentative / Decline with "Send the response now" prompt.

## 4. Common actions
- **Reply** sends to the sender only; **Reply all** sends to everyone on To and Cc; **Forward** sends the thread plus attachments to new people and drops the original recipients.
- **Archive** (Gmail `e`, Outlook Backspace) removes from Inbox without deleting; **Delete** moves to Trash, purged after 30 days.
- **Label / Move to folder**: files the thread; in a shared mailbox everyone sees the same label or folder.
- **Star / Flag**: marks for follow-up; Outlook flags show up in To Do and on the Tasks list. **Mark complete** removes the flag.
- **Snooze** (Gmail) or **Flag with a date** (Outlook): hides or reminds; the message reappears at the top at that time.
- **Mark as unread**, **Mark as read**, **Mute** (Gmail, stops a noisy thread from returning to Inbox).
- **Create filter / rule**: condition (from, subject contains, to a shared address) and action (apply label, skip Inbox, forward, mark important). Rules run only on new mail unless "Also apply to matching conversations" / "Run rule now" is ticked.
- **Insert template / Quick Part**: drops boilerplate (refund approved, documents request, offer letter instructions) into the body; you still have to edit the placeholders.
- **Schedule send** (Gmail) / **Delay Delivery** (Outlook): sends later. **Undo send** (Gmail, 5 to 30 seconds) / **Recall this message** (Outlook, same tenant only, rarely works once read).
- **Request read receipt** (Outlook: Options > Request a Read Receipt), **Request delivery receipt**.
- **Attach file / Insert Drive link**; Gmail warns "Did you mean to attach files?" when the body says "attached" and nothing is.
- **Accept / Tentative / Decline** on an invite: writes it to your calendar and emails the organiser; **Propose new time** (Outlook).
- **Add to Tasks**, **Create event from message**, **Print**, **Show original / View message source** for headers.

## 5. Where mistakes happen
- Reply all to a thread that includes the customer, the supplier or the candidate, with internal commentary still in the body ("they're being difficult, just refund it"). Check every address chip before Send; Bcc the external party if the internal thread must continue.
- Replying from your personal address instead of the shared mailbox, so the customer answers you, the thread leaves the queue, and nobody covers it when you are out. Set the From / "Send as" to the shared address.
- Forwarding a thread with an earlier attachment that contains someone else's bank details, salary, or ID scan. Delete the attachment and trim the quoted history before forwarding.
- Trusting the display name on an invoice or bank-change email. "Sarah Chen <sarah.chen@acme-pay.co>" is not the Sarah Chen you know; expand the address and check the External tag, then verify by phone.
- Archiving or snoozing a message in the shared mailbox: it disappears for the whole team. Use a label or folder (`Waiting on supplier`) instead so others can see it is parked.
- Filters that skip the Inbox on broad conditions (`subject contains invoice`), so a disputed-invoice reply or a legal notice lands unseen in a label nobody opens.
- Accepting a calendar invite without "Send the response now", so the organiser's RSVP list still shows you as Awaiting; or declining a recurring series when you meant only one occurrence.
- Sending the template with placeholders still in it (`[Customer name]`, `[amount]`, `<<start date>>`), or with last week's recipient's details because you reused a draft.
- Marking a thread as read while skimming on the phone, then losing it; the queue is "unread = untouched" and a read, unlabelled message looks done.
- Hitting Send with the attachment chip showing "Uploading..." or a Drive link that the recipient does not have access to ("You need permission").
